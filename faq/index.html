CoolTouch for Mac / FAQ

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you make it touch.

These answers describe CoolTouch for Mac 2.2.1 (Build 345), a Mac menu-bar app that adds taps, scrolling and multi-touch gestures on compatible external USB touchscreens. They follow the same official wording as the CoolTouch homepage.

Why do I need CoolTouch for an external touchscreen?

A screen can display your Mac correctly while touch does not respond. Video and touch input are separate: connecting a display does not guarantee macOS can use its touch controller. CoolTouch adds supported touch input and gestures for compatible external USB touchscreens. It does not add touch to a MacBook’s built-in screen.

What are the system requirements?

CoolTouch 2.2.1 requires an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later), macOS 15 or later, and a compatible external USB touchscreen. Both video and USB touch input must reach the Mac. Some USB-C cables carry both.

Using macOS 14? Keep your compatible version. The 2.2.1 update requires macOS 15 or later. See compatibility for the full wording.

Will it work with any touchscreen?

Compatibility depends on the touchscreen controller and its input format. CoolTouch checks the touch input and can guide you through supported alternatives, but we do not promise universal compatibility. Not every controller or input format is supported.

If your screen is unverified, try the 7-day full-feature trial on your own Mac, or ask us at support@cooltouch.app before buying.

Does CoolTouch add touch to a MacBook’s built-in screen?

No. CoolTouch does not add touch to a MacBook’s built-in screen. It is for compatible external USB touchscreens connected to an Apple silicon Mac.

Do I have to choose a screen or calibrate every time?

No. Matching saved bindings are reused. Confirmation may be needed when screen identity is ambiguous. Calibration is available when touch alignment needs adjustment.

Can I change how touch feels?

CoolTouch provides Everyday, Precise control, and Reading & browsing presets. You can undo a preset switch. You can also calibrate the touchscreen when touch alignment needs adjustment. Gesture support depends on the device, the app and the touch area.

Which gestures work in my apps?

In Chrome, pinch over text to zoom the whole page. On supported embedded maps, pinch inside the map to zoom it while the surrounding page stays the same size. Pinch a PDF in Preview to zoom smoothly. In Photos, open a photo and pinch to change its zoom level in steps. In Apple Maps, turn two fingers together to rotate the map. In Music, slide up or down through a list, or sideways through a scrollable row of albums.

In Finder, tap to click, double-tap to open, and use supported long-press menus and dragging. Gesture behavior depends on the app, region and your macOS settings. Apple Maps two-finger rotation is distinct from pinch-to-zoom on supported embedded maps in Chrome.

Can I scroll and select text with one finger?

In Preview, swipe with one finger to scroll a PDF. To select text, tap a word, then touch it again and drag across the text. In Finder, press and hold a file for its menu. Keep your finger down and drag to move the item.

What if my screen doesn’t respond?

The touch check includes a clear “didn’t respond” option. Where supported, CoolTouch tries another mode. If matching remains unconfirmed, you can retry or save a support report for troubleshooting. The troubleshooting page collects those official recovery steps. You can also email support@cooltouch.app.

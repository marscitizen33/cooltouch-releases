## English posts ready to adapt

**Intro with the real demo**

Your Mac shows a picture on your external touchscreen. But what happens when you tap it? CoolTouch adds taps, scrolling and gestures to compatible USB touchscreens. Watch it in action, then try it on your own screen for 7 days. No card required. https://cooltouch.app/

**Setup with the wizard image**

Picture working, touch missing? Check that your display sends USB touch input as well as video. CoolTouch’s setup guides you through a one-finger swipe and a two-finger check, then asks you to pick a display when needed. Try it on your Mac: https://cooltouch.app/

**Workflow for Mac users**

Reading a PDF in Preview? Swipe the page to scroll, then spread two fingers to zoom. In Finder, hold a file to open its menu, then drag to move it. See the touchscreen demo: https://cooltouch.app/#real-demo

| Photos | Pinch to zoom an open photo in steps. |

Keep Apple Maps rotation distinct from Chrome map zoom. Compatibility depends on the controller, the app and the area touched. For a specific unverified screen, invite the owner to use the free trial. Avoid universal compatibility claims, Apple endorsement, invented testimonials and claims of native continuous zoom in Photos.

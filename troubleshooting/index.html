CoolTouch for Mac / Troubleshooting

CLEAR RECOVERY

If touch isn’t confirmed, start here.

CoolTouch checks available touch input first. When it needs your help, the next step is clear. The guidance below repeats the official setup and recovery wording from the homepage and the 2.2.1 release notes. It does not add unofficial diagnostics.

Confirm that video and USB touch input both reach the Mac

Connect video and USB touch input. Some screens carry both over a single USB-C cable. Your screen must send supported touch input over USB. A video cable alone isn’t enough.

CoolTouch 2.2.1 also requires an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. It does not add touch to a MacBook’s built-in screen. If you are unsure whether your controller is compatible, see compatibility, try the 7-day full-feature trial, or email support@cooltouch.app before buying.

Follow the guided touch check

CoolTouch’s setup guides you through a short, guided touch check when one is needed. Follow the clear single-finger and two-finger instructions. There’s no countdown to rush you.

The touch check includes a clear “didn’t respond” option. Where supported, CoolTouch tries another mode.

Choose your display, or adjust alignment, only if needed

Use your mouse to confirm the touchscreen when needed. Confirmation may be needed when screen identity is ambiguous. Matching saved bindings are reused, so you do not have to choose a screen or calibrate every time.

Adjust alignment only if it needs it. Calibration is available when touch alignment needs adjustment.

If matching remains unconfirmed

If touch isn’t confirmed, follow the guided check, review a support report or retry when ready. If matching remains unconfirmed, you can retry or save a support report for troubleshooting, then email support@cooltouch.app.

Install, Accessibility permission, and activation

Unzip the download, move CoolTouch to Applications and open it. Grant Accessibility permission when prompted, then follow any setup instructions. Activate with the license key from your Gumroad receipt.

If you can’t open the app, use the download and license key in your original Gumroad purchase.

Updates, macOS 14, and existing licenses

On an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 15 or later, open CoolTouch in the menu bar and choose Check for Updates. Upgrading from 2.2.0 keeps your screen mapping, calibration and gesture settings. Your existing license includes this update.

Using macOS 14? Keep your compatible version. The 2.2.1 update requires macOS 15 or later. Read the 2.2.1 release notes.

Replacing a touchscreen

If two screens of the same model use the same USB port, CoolTouch can distinguish their failed touch checks when the controller provides a usable USB serial number. This lets a replacement screen try a mode that failed on the previous screen. When the display mapping is ambiguous, CoolTouch asks which screen you touched.

Contact support

If you need help after the steps above, email support@cooltouch.app. You can include a support report when you have one. Unsure about your setup? Ask us before buying.
